System Core

Direct ViGEmBus Telemetry

Kernel Emulation

Virtual Xbox 360 Controller

Direct communication with the Windows Virtual Gamepad Emulation Bus (`ViGEmBus`). Forza Horizon, Steam, and Windows recognize the device as an authentic Xbox 360 gamepad with zero configuration required.

Telemetry Engine

All Horizon Games Auto-Detect

The Windows Companion continuously monitors process execution for Forza Horizon 5, Forza Horizon 4, Forza Horizon 3, Forza Horizon 6, and Forza Motorsport, dynamically synchronizing telemetry profiles.

FastStream Protocol

120Hz Binary Datagrams

Low-overhead binary UDP stream transmitting analog steering, brake, throttle, and digital button states directly between phone and PC with sub-millisecond latency.

Quick Start

Three-Step Deployment

Step 01

Run Windows Companion

Launch `fh6-controller-windows.exe` on your PC. It auto-verifies ViGEmBus, starts the 120Hz UDP listener on port 4242, and generates your local pairing QR code.

Step 02

Scan QR From Phone

Open FH6 Controller on your Android phone and tap the scanner icon. Point your phone camera at the PC screen QR code to sync network parameters automatically.

Step 03

Drive in Forza Horizon

Launch Forza Horizon. The virtual Xbox 360 controller is already active and mapped. Drive with dynamic thumb steering or 6-axis gyro tilt immediately.
