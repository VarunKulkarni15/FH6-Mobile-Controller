1. Zero Data Collection Architecture

HRZN Mobile Controller (including the Windows Companion application, the Android application, and related utilities) is architected with strict, privacy-by-design principles:

We collect zero personal data. No tracking cookies, no device telemetry, no personal identifiers, no analytics beacons, and no diagnostic metrics are collected, logged, stored, or transmitted to any third-party server or cloud endpoint.

The software functions entirely offline and directly between your devices over your private Local Area Network (LAN).

2. Network Communication & Telemetry Stream

All data transmitted between the Android mobile client and the Windows host companion occurs exclusively over your local WiFi/Ethernet network:

  • Protocol: Direct 120Hz binary UDP datagrams transmitted on local port 4242.
  • Payload: Real-time controller axis inputs (steering thumb position, accelerator pressure, brake pressure, and controller button states).
  • Scope: Datagrams never leave your local subnet and are never relayed through intermediate servers, proxies, or cloud routers.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Access

The mobile and Windows applications request only permissions strictly required for real-time gamepad emulation:

  • Camera Access (Mobile): Used strictly by the Google Play Services Code Scanner to scan the local pairing QR code displayed on your PC screen. Camera frames are processed strictly in-memory on the device and are never saved, recorded, or transmitted.
  • Local Network / Wi-Fi State: Required to establish the peer-to-peer UDP connection between the phone and PC.
  • Motion Sensors / Gyroscope: Processed in real time for tilt-based steering angle calculation. Sensor readings are processed immediately and discarded with zero persistence.
  • Vibration / Haptics: Provides tactile feedback during button presses and simulated pedal resistance.
